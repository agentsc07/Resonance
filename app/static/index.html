Details

Real voices, controlled flaws, known truth.

We start from clean recordings of real speakers, then change one thing at a time: a rushed phrase, a misplaced pause, a skipped word. Because we made each change, we know exactly where it is.

What we change

Fifteen kinds of delivery flaw, in seven areas, each at five strengths from barely there to egregious.

Recording conditions

Every voice also appears in rougher recordings, to check that a bad microphone is not mistaken for bad delivery.

Speakers

Eight studio voices and two excerpts of a 1962 speech.
Manifest

Spoken delivery, measured against a known truth.

Flawline injects controlled flaws into real recordings, then asks an engine to find them blind: where each one is, what it is, and what it costs. Because the truth is known, every number below can be checked.
