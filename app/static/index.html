Details

Real voices with flaws we added on purpose.

We start with clean recordings of real speakers, then change one thing at a time: speed up a phrase, add a pause in the wrong place, remove a word. Because we made every change ourselves, we know exactly where and how strong it is.

What we change

The 15 kinds of delivery flaw we add, grouped into 7 areas. Each comes in 5 strengths, from barely noticeable to extreme.

Recording conditions

Each voice is also re-recorded with noise, echo, a phone line and compression, to check that a poor microphone is never mistaken for poor delivery.

Speakers

The voices behind the clips: eight studio speakers and two excerpts of a 1962 speech. Some are kept aside for testing.
Manifest

Delivery, scored with evidence.

Flawline finds the exact moments a reading slips (a rushed phrase, a misplaced pause, a skipped word), shows what each one cost, and lets you hear it. It was built and checked on real recordings with flaws we added ourselves, so the answer is always known.
