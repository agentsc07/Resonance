## JFK Rice University address, 12 Sep 1962: baselines B09-B10 and every clip derived from them
Two ~60 s excerpts of President Kennedy's "Address at Rice University on the Nation's Space Effort". US Government work, **public domain**
(JFK Library recording JFKWHA-127-002). Obtained from Rice University's copy on the Internet Archive (item `president-john-f.-kennedy-09-12-1962`,
Public Domain Mark 1.0), https://archive.org/details/president-john-f.-kennedy-09-12-1962. Changes made: two excerpts cut, silence trimmed to 0.3 s margins,
resampled to 22.05 kHz mono, one gain change; altered variants are derivative works (no restrictions). The full speech is not redistributed here.
Split `extra`: never used to fit or select anything. A 1962 open-air recording (crowd and PA noise, rhetorical pauses), not studio quality.
